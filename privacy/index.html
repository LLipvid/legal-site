Lipvid Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

Your device, your journal

Lipvid stores your profile name, saved shades, likes, comments, messages, imported videos, photos and creations on your device. No account or developer-operated server is required. We do not upload your photos, videos, facial landmarks or conversations. Facial landmarks are processed transiently on your device for a photo tint and are not stored as a face profile.

Permissions

Lipvid asks for permission before accessing the camera, adding creations to Photos or enabling notifications. The system photo picker only provides the media you select. You may decline permission and continue using other features. Daily reminders are scheduled locally.

Purchases and third-party services

Apple processes in-app purchases under its own privacy practices. Lipvid stores verified transaction identifiers and your remaining Slice balance locally to prevent duplicate delivery. Lipvid does not receive payment card details. Your journal media is not sent to third-party services. Sharing uses the destination you choose; only share when you agree to that destination's handling of your data.

Remote legal pages

Opening these pages contacts the web hosting provider, which may receive ordinary connection information such as an IP address and browser request information. Doya includes no advertising or tracking SDK.

Retention and deletion

You can delete a creation or clear journal data in Profile. Clearing the journal preserves the balance and purchase ledger. Removing the app may delete locally stored content and remaining Slices. Device backups, if enabled by you, are governed by your device backup settings. Exported copies in Photos or other apps must be deleted separately.

Contact

Contact address pending activation: LLipvid.github.io.contact@gmail.com.

Content controls

Report, block, hide and word-filter controls are available for videos, conversations and comments. Blocking hides a creator across these areas on this device. Reports and filtering choices are stored locally. Reports are not automatically transmitted; use Share report in Content controls to send a report for review. Do not submit harassment, threats, hate, explicit material or spam. Reported reference material can be removed from future catalog releases after review. Contact: LLipvid.github.io.contact@gmail.com.
